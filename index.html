Build anything. Explore everywhere.

A relaxing block-building game where your town comes alive. Build houses and roads, then jump into a car, a train or a plane and explore what you made.

Get it on Google Play
Free on Android.

What you can do

Build your town

Lots of blocks, real furniture, guided house plans, undo and redo, and fly mode.

Drive, ride and fly

Cars, taxis, trains on tracks you lay yourself, helicopters, planes, jets, boats, horses and a roller coaster.

Grow a village

Villagers move into the houses you build, ask for help and earn you coins.

Explore new worlds

Forests, mountains and deserts, a Japan world with Mount Fuji, and an ocean full of fish.

Pets and skins

Adopt a dog, a cat or a chicken for free, and unlock new looks for your character.

Your worlds are safe

Optional Google sign-in keeps your worlds in the cloud so you can carry on on another phone.
