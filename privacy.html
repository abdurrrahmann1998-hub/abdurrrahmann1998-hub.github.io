BlockTown 3D – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

BlockTown 3D ("the game") is made by ToTo Company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is meant for players aged 13 and over.

What we collect

Player account. When you start the game, Unity Authentication gives you an anonymous player ID. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, we receive an ID from that service so you can use the same account on other devices. Facebook Login is not currently offered; if it is added in a future update, we will receive a Facebook ID only if you choose to sign in with it. We do not receive your password, and we do not see your contacts or friends list.

Game saves. Your worlds, progress, coins, gems, purchases you own and settings are stored on your device and, when you are signed in, in Unity Cloud Save so you can restore them later.

Gameplay statistics (only if you agree). On first launch the game asks whether you want to share anonymous gameplay statistics, such as which world types are played, tutorial progress, and how many blocks are placed. These are sent to Unity Analytics together with basic device information (device model, operating system, country, app version). You can change your answer at any time in Settings > "Share usage data".

Ads. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and information about your device to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your Android settings. Buying "Remove Ads" turns ads off.

Purchases. In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; we only receive confirmation of what you bought so we can give it to you.

Crash reports. If the game crashes, Google Play and Unity may collect a technical crash report to help us fix the problem.

How we use it

  • To run the game, save and restore your progress, and deliver what you buy.
  • To understand how the game is played and improve it (only with your consent).
  • To show ads in the free version.

We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Remote Config, Analytics) – privacy policy
  • Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing – privacy policy
  • Google AdMob – how Google uses data in ads
  • Facebook Login – not currently offered; if added in a future update, used only if you choose it – privacy policy

Keeping and deleting your data

Your cloud data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all cloud data at any time in the game: Pause menu > Account & Saves > Delete account. If you can no longer open the game, email us at the address below from the email linked to your account (or include your Player ID, shown in Account & Saves) and we will delete it within 30 days. See Delete your account. Analytics data is kept for up to 2 years and is not linked to your name.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Significant changes will also be shown in the game.
