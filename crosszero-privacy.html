Cross Zero — Tic Tac Toe – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Cross Zero — Tic Tac Toe ("the game") is made by ToTo Company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

No account, no personal details

The game has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email, contacts or location. Your scores, settings and the items you own are stored only on your device.

Ads

The free version shows a banner ad and, only when you choose to watch one, rewarded video ads, through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about your device and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > "Privacy choices". You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Buying "Remove Ads" turns the banner off.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (board themes, mark styles and Remove Ads) are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; the game only receives confirmation of what you bought so it can unlock it.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, Google Play may collect a technical crash report to help us fix the problem.

Services we use

  • Google AdMob – how Google uses data in ads
  • Google Play Billing – Google privacy policy

We do not sell your personal information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete what we can.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
