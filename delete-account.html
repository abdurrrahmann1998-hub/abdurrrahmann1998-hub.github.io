Delete your BlockTown 3D account

BlockTown 3D is made by ToTo Company. You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open BlockTown 3D and start or load any world.
  2. Tap MENU to open the pause menu.
  3. Tap Account & Saves, then Delete account, and confirm.

Your account and cloud data are deleted straight away.

By email

If you can no longer open the game, email tgiant509@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my BlockTown 3D account". Send it from the email linked to your Google or Facebook sign-in, or include your Player ID (shown in Account & Saves). We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your player account and sign-in link (Google Play Games or Facebook).
  • Your cloud saves: worlds, progress, coins, gems, owned items and settings.

What is kept

  • Anonymous gameplay statistics (only collected if you agreed) are kept for up to 2 years and are not linked to your name.
  • Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases under Google's policies. Deleting your account does not refund purchases.

Worlds saved only on your phone stay there until you uninstall the game.
